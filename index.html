Zesty Swap

Swap. Match. Squeeze!

Closed testing

The juiciest match-3 puzzle in the orchard. Swap neighbouring fruit to line up three or more, set off chain reactions with fruit blasters, and squeeze every point out of 50 handcrafted levels.

  • Easy to pick up: swipe or tap to swap, cascades for bonus points
  • Line Blasters, bombs and combos for big splashy turns
  • Hints and auto-reshuffle so you never get stuck

Storm Saucer

Drag. Dodge. Destroy the boss.

Coming soon

Pilot a flying saucer under a raging storm. Just drag to steer, and lift your finger to hover. Fly through eight hand-drawn biomes, each a level with its own hazards and a themed boss waiting at the end, or chase high scores in Endless.

  • 8 biome levels with bosses: Arctic Peaks, Pine Wilds, Ember Dunes, War Zone, Shark Sea, Neon Skyline, Toxic Wastes and Crystal Caverns
  • 10 alien weapons (laser, homing missiles, spread, chain lightning, plasma, saw, orbital strike, flamethrower, freeze ray, black hole) with FIRE and SWAP buttons
  • 10 animated alien pilots, legendary saucer skins and Hangar upgrades
  • Bosses aim at you, so keep moving and keep firing

Trail Torque

Climb. Upgrade. Conquer.

Coming soon

A physics hill-climb racer with real suspension. Balance on the gas and brake to crawl over ridges, plough through mud bogs, skate across ice and fly off mega ramps, then spend your coins on engines, tyres, shocks and NOS.

  • 10 vehicles from Common to Legendary, from the Trail Scout to the Coffin Cruiser
  • 12 drivers with their own perks, riding in the cab and tumbling as ragdolls
  • 9 maps: Pine Greenway, Red Rock, Frostline, Moon, Volcanic, Sky Jump, Mega Ramp, Speed Run and Bog
  • Checkpoints that refuel, repair and refill NOS, plus jump and flip bonuses
