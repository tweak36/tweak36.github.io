Trail Torque

Climb. Upgrade. Conquer.

In testing

A physics hill-climb racer with real suspension. Balance gas and brake over ridges, through mud bogs, across ice and off mega ramps, then spend your coins on engines, tyres, shocks and NOS. Not released yet: Trail Torque is in testing on Google Play and Apple TestFlight ahead of launch.

  • 14 maps, from Pine Greenway to the Haunted Graveyard, plus a hands-on Driving School tutorial
  • 16 vehicles and 18 drivers, including the Bone Rattler, Warhog Tank and the Headless Horseman
  • Equipment kits for road, mud, ice and tracks, plus upgrades and vehicle mastery
  • Offline cups against computer racers, ghost racing, trick popups, daily streaks and missions

Zesty Swap

Swap. Match. Squeeze!

In development

The juiciest match-3 puzzle in the orchard. Swap neighbouring fruit to line up three or more, set off chain reactions with fruit blasters, and squeeze every point out of 50 handcrafted levels.

  • Swipe or tap to swap, with cascades for bonus points
  • Line Blasters, bombs and combos for big splashy turns
  • Hints and auto-reshuffle so you never get stuck

Storm Saucer

Drag. Dodge. Destroy the boss.

In development

Pilot a flying saucer under a raging storm. Drag to steer, lift your finger to hover, and fly through eight hand-drawn biomes, each with its own hazards and a themed boss at the end. Or chase high scores in Endless.

  • 8 biome levels with bosses, from Arctic Peaks to Crystal Caverns
  • 10 alien weapons, from laser and chain lightning to a black hole
  • 10 animated alien pilots, legendary saucer skins and Hangar upgrades
All screenshots

PennyPing

Track every penny.

In development

A clean, private subscription tracker. See what you really spend each month, get a ping before every renewal, and cancel the ones you forgot about. Works fully offline, with no account required.

  • Monthly and yearly spend at a glance
  • Renewal reminders you control
  • Light and dark themes, plus quick-add for popular services

About

A small studio with a clumsy robot mascot

TWEAKFAILS is an independent game and app studio based in North Carolina, USA.

We own and develop Trail Torque (in testing) along with Zesty Swap, Storm Saucer and PennyPing (in development), and we produce the TWEAKFAILS YouTube channel. Each game gets its own art style, from painterly off-road landscapes to juicy fruit and stormy alien skies.
