Trail Torque Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Trail Torque ("the game") is developed by Tweak (developer) ("we"). This policy explains what information the game and its third-party partners collect.

Information we collect

We don't run our own servers and we don't ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location. Your game progress (best distances, coins, vehicles, drivers, upgrades, unlocked maps and settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on the map-select screen only (never while driving), an occasional full-screen ad between races (at most every third race), and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for bonus coins or to double a run's coins. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob's SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, only with permission on iOS)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)
  • ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic or crash information about the ads

Google may use this data to show personalised ads where you have consented and the law allows it. Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), we ask for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. You can change that choice in the game's consent form, reset or opt out of your advertising ID in your device settings, or buy Remove Ads. Learn how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites · Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (coin packs and Remove Ads) are processed by Google Play; we never receive your payment details. The game uses Google Play Billing on your device to complete, acknowledge and restore purchases (Remove Ads is restored automatically when you reinstall). Google Play privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Device features

The game uses vibration (haptics) for feedback and plays sounds; it does not use the microphone, camera, contacts or precise location. Vibration and sound can be turned off in Settings.

Children

Trail Torque isn't directed at children under 13 and is intended for a general audience aged 13+. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we'll help delete it with our partners.

Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game. Ad and purchase data is kept by Google under its own policies. Contact us with any deletion request.

Security

Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Changes

We'll update this page and the date above if this policy changes.
