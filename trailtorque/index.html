Trail Torque

Trail Torque

Junkyard robot offroad racer for iPhone and Android, by TWEAKFAILS.

Meet Tweak, a scrappy little robot with a garage full of junk. Tweak's first ride is the Junk Bug: rusty, trusty and held together with tape. Drive it, upgrade it and turn it into the fully restored Torque Bug. Then haul home the Bolt Hauler, a six-wheel scrap truck you restore piece by piece with every upgrade.

What's inside

  • 19 vehicles and 19 drivers, from the Junk Bug and Bolt Hauler to the Bone Rattler and the Headless Horseman
  • 14 hand-painted maps, from Pine Greenway and Ember Caldera to City Rooftops and the Haunted Graveyard
  • Equipment kits for every surface: Road Tires, Mud Claws, Ice Studs and a full Track Kit, plus a raised snorkel
  • Upgrades, vehicle mastery, a Paint Shop and saved setups
  • Driving School with Tweak, offline cups against computer rivals, ghost racing, daily rewards, missions and 12 trophies
  • Plays offline, no account needed

Price

Free to play. Everything can be earned with coins collected by driving. Optional in-app purchases are available, and the game shows ads that can be removed with a one-time purchase. Rewarded videos are always optional.

Availability

Trail Torque is in testing ahead of launch on the App Store and Google Play. Store links will appear here at launch.
