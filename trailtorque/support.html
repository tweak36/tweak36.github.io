Support

Trail Torque

Trail Torque™ Support

Need help with Trail Torque? Email support@tweakfails.com and we'll get back to you, usually within two business days. Please include your device model, the game version (shown on the main menu and in Settings > About & Support) and what happened.

Purchases

I bought something but didn't get it. Make sure you're online, then open the Shop and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. Permanent items (vehicles, drivers, maps, paint packs, bundles and Remove Ads) are restored to the same Apple ID or Google account. Coin packs and flip resets are consumable and are added once, when the purchase completes.

Moving to a new phone. Game progress is saved on your device only. Permanent purchases come back with RESTORE PURCHASES on the new device; coins, upgrades and unlocks earned with coins don't transfer.

Refunds. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com (App Store) or through your Google Play order history.

Ads

A "Watch ad" button says no ad is available. Ads load in the background and need an internet connection. Wait a moment and try again. If you declined personalised ads, fewer ads may be available in your region.

Removing ads. The Remove Ads purchase, the Titan Starter pack and any purchase over $4.99 remove the banner and between-race ads. Optional rewarded videos stay available if you want the bonus.

Ad privacy choices. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), change your ad consent any time from Settings > Ad Privacy Choices. On iPhone you can also turn off tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Gameplay

Replay the tutorial. Open Settings from the main menu and tap TUTORIAL.

My vehicle is stuck upside down. Use a flip reset, or end the run from the pause menu and retry from your last checkpoint.
